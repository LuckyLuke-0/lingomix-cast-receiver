Trevuxa Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 September 2026

Trevuxa is an independent media utility. It opens a compatible public video link supplied by the user, lets the user select available video, audio and subtitle languages independently, plays those remote sources and can send the selected media to a Google Cast device.

Developer and contact

Trevuxa is published by its independent developer under the Trevuxa name. Privacy questions can be submitted through the public support page at GitHub support.

Data handled by Trevuxa

No account is required. Trevuxa does not include advertising, developer-operated analytics, Firebase, tracking profiles or a developer-operated backend that receives viewing history. The app processes the URL you choose and the media metadata returned by the relevant public content host. App-language, theme, quality and subtitle-style preferences are stored locally on your Android device.

Network requests and third parties

Opening or playing a link necessarily sends network requests to the content host serving that link. The host therefore receives ordinary connection data such as your IP address and request metadata under its own privacy terms. When you choose Cast, Google Cast services and the Cast device you select receive the media URL, title/playback metadata and the information needed to play the stream. Trevuxa does not sell or use this information for advertising.

Temporary media

Normal playback streams media from the remote host. When independently selected video and audio tracks cannot be sent as one direct Cast stream, Trevuxa may create a temporary remuxed MP4 in the app's private Android cache and expose it only on the local network for the active Cast session. It is not uploaded to a Trevuxa server. Android may retain cached files until they are replaced, cleared by the app or operating system, the app's storage is cleared, or the app is uninstalled.

Permissions

Internet and network-state access are used to resolve links, stream media and connect to Cast devices. Notification permission, when requested on supported Android versions, is used for Cast playback controls. Trevuxa does not request contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, SMS, call logs or storage-library access.

Retention and deletion

Preferences and temporary app-cache data remain on the device only. You can reset preferences in the app and remove all Trevuxa local data through Android's app-storage controls or by uninstalling the app. Trevuxa has no remote user account or developer database containing this data.

Children

Trevuxa is a general-purpose media utility and is not specifically directed to children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third-party content and independence

Trevuxa does not bundle, host or claim ownership of third-party media. Users must follow the terms and rights that apply to the source they open. Trevuxa is not an official application of, affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by any third-party content provider.

Changes

This privacy policy will be updated if the app's data handling materially changes. The effective date above identifies the current version.
